### I was a homestuck now!

Since then I have made fanart, made my own fankids and fantrolls, read fan-ventures from [MSPFA](mspfa.com/), [and have even made my own!](https://mspfa.com/?s=63438&p=1)

But if you asked me anything about the comic, the alchemy, the dancestors, or the classpecting, I would have a hard time answering.

Because the truth is, I didn't actually read the comic. I skimmed through the pesterlogs, skipped reading the descriptions, and I even skipped a big important flash! I wasnt a fan, I was a LARPER (sobs)!!!! 

But that changes today! (or about 3 days ago)

3 days ago, I had began to read Homestuck in my downtime, and REALLY read it this time! I was gonna read ALL THE PESTERLOGS and ALL OF THE FLASHES and go through ALL OF THE SYLLADEXES AND CAPTCHALOGS AND ALL OF THE SUCH.

I am currently on **ACT 5, page 2085**, here are my many thoughts:

# Misc. thoughts

- My favorite parts so far are all the alchemy montages, it's just so fun to watch the kids make complete bullshit.

- [My favorite joke](https://www.homestuck.com/003503) was when Terezi slaps Karkat, then later John tells past Terezi about the whole ordeal, which causes her to slap Karkat in the first place

- Man sometimes it can be easy to get lost or confused when youre reading this, at times, I'll wonder how this character ended up like this, or how this character got this item, but I usually go back to reread anything i missed.

- I love how everything connects in this comic. I dont know whether Hussie had a whole plan about this, but whether or not he does, he is very good at connecting things that may seem meaningless at first. Is that what foreshadowing is?

- The dynamics with the trolls so far is also something to write home about! They really seem to hate each other on the surface, but they also genuinely care for each other! I especially liked the conversation with Karkat and Terezi

- I really wish there were comments on the homestuck website cause I wonder how people reacted to seeing the designs for the trolls, and i wonder how people discussed the pages when they were coming out. I hope I can find some threads on some archives of the mspa forums.
